61: Build the Coalition
Corrected coalition developments update
30 October 2026

Changes:
1. Joint List remains visible but is treated as OUTSIDE SUPPORT ONLY for the minority government route.
2. Yisrael Beiteinu no longer has an automatic veto against Ra'am. It still rejects the Joint List, Shas and UTJ and refuses Netanyahu.
3. Ra'am can therefore remain a possible coalition or outside support partner with Yisrael Beiteinu, but this does not by itself create a majority.
4. The reported UTJ role is abstention from outside government, not coalition membership.
5. The Yashar + B'Yachad + Democrats route can reach 60 confidence votes with Joint List and Ra'am outside support. With 7 UTJ abstentions, the confidence arithmetic becomes 60 in favour to 53 against.
6. This route is explicitly labelled mathematically possible but politically extremely fragile.
7. If Yisrael Beiteinu is included, Joint List support is unavailable. Even with Ra'am support and 7 UTJ abstentions, the route remains short.
8. Final seat allocation and image assets are unchanged.
