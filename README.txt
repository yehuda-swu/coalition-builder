61: Build the Coalition
Coalition sensitive minority government update
12 October 2026

Changes:
Minority government route is now available only to a Yashar led coalition in the game scenario.
Ra'am may be inside that coalition or provide external support.
Joint Arab List may abstain from a Yashar led confidence vote.
Likud led and other non Yashar led minority coalitions cannot use Arab party support or abstention as an arithmetic shortcut.
An explanatory message appears for substantial under 61 coalitions where the minority route is unavailable.

All existing polling, Preferred Prime Minister cards, Coalition Strength and Ra'am stability rules are retained.
